# Peter Soul Newsletter

This archive contains the columns written by Peter Soul for the Thames Valley Group of Advanced Motorists newsletter, published between July 2002 and March 2019. The columns, titled "A Physicist Writes...", cover motoring topics from a physicist's perspective, including:

- Vehicle technology and testing
- Driverless cars and collision-avoidance systems
- Advanced driving techniques
- Tyres, road conditions, and motorway physics
- Off-topic pieces on French lessons, stereoscopic photos, litter-picking, cycling, the Olympics, electricity supply voltages, cancer, children's education, exoplanets, and walking holidays

The columns are personal opinion and do not represent the views of the Thames Valley Group or the IAM.

## Archive

This repository is an archive of the Peter Soul website originally published at petersoul.co.uk. It contains the full collection of newsletter columns, HTML pages, images, and related content documenting 17 years of motoring commentary and occasional excursions into physics, neuroscience, and everyday observations.

## Purpose

The archive preserves the newsletter posts written between July 2002 and March 2019. The content has been backdated to reflect its original publication timeline.
