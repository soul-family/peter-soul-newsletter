# About the Archive

This repository preserves the blog columns written by the author between 2002 and 2019.

## Archive Purpose

The archive exists to preserve the original published content exactly as authored, maintaining the wording, design intent, and structure of the original site.

## Audience

The archive is intended for researchers, and anyone interested in the historical content.

## What You Will Find

- Newsletter columns posts from July 2002 to March 2019
- Personal notes, letters, and other content
- Original images and assets

## Structure

- **Blog posts** — located in `content/columns/`, organized by publish date
- **Pages** — located in `content/`, including notes, letters, and links
- **Assets** — located in `assets/`, including images and diagrams

## Separate Repositories

The family tree content is managed in a separate repository. See the `_docs/family-tree/archive-guide.md` for more details.

## Dates

Each page includes a `Latest Updated on` annotation at the bottom. This shows when the content was last revised in the archive.

## Images

Click any image to view it at full size. Some family tree pages include thumbnail images that link to larger versions.

## External Links

Links to external websites open in a new tab or window. The archive does not maintain or control external content.

## Browser Compatibility

The archive works best in modern browsers: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge. JavaScript is not required for basic navigation.

You are free to share and adapt the content for non-commercial purposes, with attribution to Peter Soul and petersoul.co.uk.

## Contact

For archive-related enquiries, please use the contact details provided by the archive maintainers. The original author is no longer available, but enquiries can be made through his daughter.

## Structure

```
content/columns/
├── index.html          # Blog archive index (links.html)
├── july_2002.html
├── august_2002.html
├── ...
└── march_2019.html
```

## Navigation

- The `index.html` provides a chronological archive of all blog posts.
- Each post includes a `Latest Updated on` annotation.
- Posts are linked from the main site navigation.

## Content Preservation

- Original author wording is preserved exactly as published.
- Encoding has been recovered from cp1252/latin-1 to UTF-8.
- Grammar and spelling are untouched.
