# Submit New Info

## Overview

This guide explains how to submit additional information to the Soul family tree archive, You can contribute corrections, additions, or new content.

## Before You Submit

### Check First

Before submitting new information:

- Browse the Archive to see if the information already exists
- Check the family tree guide for family-related content
- Review the browsing guide to understand the current structure

### What You Can Submit

- Corrections to blog post text
- Context about events mentioned in posts
- Background information about people or places mentioned
- Date corrections
- Relationship clarifications
- Additional biographical details
- Source documents or references

### What You Cannot Submit

- Personal private information about living people without permission
- Unverified rumors or speculation
- Copyrighted material without permission
- Content unrelated to the family tree archive

## How to Submit

### Step 1: Gather Your Information

Prepare your submission with:

- **What**: The specific information you want to add or correct
- **Where**: Which page, post, or family tree branch it relates to
- **Source**: Where you obtained this information (if applicable)
- **Context**: Why this information is important or relevant

### Step 2: Create an Issue

1. Go to the Archive repository on GitHub
2. Click the **Issues** tab
3. Click **New issue**
4. Choose the appropriate template if available, or select **Blank issue**
5. Write a clear title, such as:
   - "Addition: Missing family member in Smith branch"
   - "Update: Corrected relationship in Wilson family tree"

### Step 3: Write Your Submission

In the issue description, include:

```
## What
Describe the information you want to submit.

## Where
Specify which page, post, or section this relates to.
- URL or file path if known
- Date or time period
- Family tree branch if applicable

## Information
Provide the actual information you want to add or correct.

## Source
Where did you get this information?
- Family records
- Personal knowledge
- Historical documents
- Other (please specify)

## Additional Context
Any other details that help understand the submission.
```

### Step 4: Submit the Issue

1. Click **Submit new issue**
2. The Archive maintainers will review your submission
3. They may ask questions or request clarification
4. If accepted, the information will be added to the Archive

## After Submission

### Review Process

1. Maintainers will review your submission
2. They may ask for more information
3. They will verify the information against existing sources
4. If accepted, it will be added in a future update

### Timeline

- Simple corrections: often within a few days
- Complex additions: may take longer for verification
- Family tree updates: require careful verification

### Notification

- You may receive notifications about your issue
- Check the issue for responses from maintainers
- The issue will be closed once the information is added or if it cannot be verified

## Best Practices

### Be Specific

- Provide exact locations (file names, dates, sections)
- Quote the existing text if suggesting a correction
- Include all relevant details in your first submission

### Provide Sources

- Documents
- Personal knowledge with context
- The more reliable the source, the faster it can be added

### Be Patient

- Maintainers review submissions carefully
- Verification takes time
- Not all submissions can be accepted if sources cannot be verified

## Privacy Considerations

When submitting information:

- Do not include private details about living people without permission
- The Archive respects privacy of family members
- Sensitive information may be redacted or omitted

## Contact

If you have questions about submitting information:

- Check existing issues to see if your question has been answered
- Use the repository's contact methods
- The Archive maintainers are happy to help
