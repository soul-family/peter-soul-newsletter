These are the external websites referred to in the columns. Where a site has moved, been reorganised or closed since the column was first published, the link now points to the page that carries the same information.

## Driving, road safety and the Highway Code

- [IAM RoadSmart](https://www.iamroadsmart.com/) - The charity that publishes the advanced driving standards referred to throughout these columns, and which supports the local car groups that many readers belong to.
- [About IAM RoadSmart](https://www.iamroadsmart.com/about-us/) - Background on the organisation, its charitable work and its history.
- [IAM RoadSmart courses for individuals](https://www.iamroadsmart.com/courses-services/for-individuals/) - Details of the advanced driving and motorcycling courses available to the public.
- [The Highway Code, rules 103 to 158](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/the-highway-code/general-rules-techniques-and-advice-for-all-drivers-and-riders-103-to-158) - The stopping distance table and the rules on speed limits, crossings and general techniques that several columns quote.
- [View your driving licence information](https://www.gov.uk/view-driving-licence) - The official service for checking the details held on a driving licence.
- [Traffic Waves](http://trafficwaves.org/) - The original account of travelling traffic waves, used to illustrate how a single driver can smooth a jam.

## Science, measurement and space

- [NASA International Space Station](https://www.nasa.gov/international-space-station/) - The station overview that replaced the retired live high-definition camera stream.
- [Heavens-Above](https://www.heavens-above.com/) - Pass predictions and the current position of the International Space Station, for seeing it with the naked eye.
- [Met Office global map data and layers](https://weather.metoffice.gov.uk/guides/global-map-data-layers) - Satellite observations, radar and lightning layers for the United Kingdom and beyond.
- [Gridwatch](https://gridwatch.templar.co.uk/) - A near real-time display of the generation and consumption of electricity on the UK grid.
- [Google Images](https://images.google.com/) - Used to find stereo pairs for the 3D viewing experiments.
- [AB Dynamics](https://www.abdynamics.com/) - The vehicle testing and robotics company whose driverless vehicle work is discussed in the final columns.
- [AB Dynamics videos](https://www.youtube.com/user/abdynamics/videos) - Video material produced by AB Dynamics.
- [Clarkson on holiday in Wales](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xP5_5RDKxOY) - The television clip referred to in the column on driving in Wales.

## Reference

- [Horizon (British TV series)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horizon_%28British_TV_series%29) - The BBC science series that made the episode on perception referred to in the columns.
- [Misinformation effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Misinformation_effect) - The research on how the wording of a question alters the accuracy of a recollection.
- [Road train](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road_train) - The heavy freight road vehicle project described in the column on preparing to board a road train.
- [Keep Britain Tidy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keep_Britain_Tidy) - The anti-litter charity whose local action groups are mentioned in the column on coasting to a halt.
- [Cinema of the United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinema_of_the_United_Kingdom) - Background on the cinema chains referred to in the column from Glyndebourne to the Paralympics.
- [Odeon Cinemas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odeon_Cinemas) - The cinema chain whose membership scheme is described in that column.
- [BBC News: the Real Story](http://news.bbc.co.uk/1/hi/programmes/real_story/4104180.stm) - The Radio 4 programme on eye witness testimony.
